Delete account and data — Global Buddy: AI Conversation

Developer (Google Play): IcepowerBR  |  Last updated: November 25, 2026

On this page you can ask us to delete your Global Buddy: AI Conversation account and the data linked to it, or to delete only some of your data without deleting the account. No login is needed.

Delete it in the App

Open the App and go to Config > Account > Delete account. Your account and its data are deleted right away, and the App clears everything saved on your phone.

Or ask us by email

  1. Email elderpv18@gmail.com from the same Google account email you use in the App.
  2. Use the subject Deletion request — Global Buddy and say whether you want to delete your account or delete only some data (and which).
  3. If you email from another address, we will ask you to confirm the account is yours.
Send request by email

If the button doesn't open your email app, send the same information to elderpv18@gmail.com.

If you have a subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Global Buddy > Cancel subscription so you are not charged again.

What we delete

  • Account data: name, email, profile photo, and Google account ID;
  • Preferences: languages, level, preferred character gender, age, and reminder settings;
  • Custom characters you created;
  • Energy balance and usage history linked to your account;
  • Feedback and reports you sent in the App, linked to your account.

We don't store voice recordings on our server, so there are none to delete. Conversation history and audio are saved only on your phone: deleting the account in the App clears them, or you can uninstall the App or clear its data in Android Settings > Apps > Global Buddy > Storage > Clear data.

What we may keep

  • Purchase and subscription records, for the period required by tax and consumer law (up to 5 years);
  • Aggregated or anonymized statistics that cannot identify you.

Data sent to advertising and measurement partners (Google AdMob, Google Firebase, Google Ads, and Meta) is linked to your device's advertising ID, not to your account, and follows those partners' retention periods. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

Timing

We confirm email requests within 7 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days after confirming the account is yours. We will email you when it's done.

More details in our Privacy Policy. By sending a request, you confirm you are the account holder or are authorized by them.
