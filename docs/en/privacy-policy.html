2. How we use information

  • To run the App: create your account, generate AI replies, transcribe your voice, translate messages, give corrections, and manage your daily energy.
  • To process and validate subscriptions and free trials.
  • To send practice reminders, if you turn them on.
  • To apply age rules, such as keeping the App unavailable to anyone under 16 and limiting data sharing for users under 18.
  • To measure usage, fix bugs, and improve the App.
  • To show ads to free users and to measure and improve our own advertising campaigns that promote the App.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and harmful content, and to comply with the law.

3. Microphone and audio

The App asks for microphone access for voice conversations. Recordings are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server to be transcribed into text and are then discarded: we do not store your voice recordings. AI reply audio and your conversation history may be saved only on your device, so you can replay them and so the AI can remember earlier conversations. You can deny microphone access and keep using the App by text.

4. How we share information

Service providers

These companies process data only to run the App on our behalf:

  • Google Cloud (server hosting and database);
  • AI providers, to generate replies, transcribe audio, and translate text;
  • Google Play (payments and subscriptions) and Google Sign-In (login).

Advertising and measurement partners

These partners receive data for their own purposes under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob shows ads to free users. We request non-personalized ads, but AdMob receives your advertising ID and device information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Meta (Facebook SDK) receives your advertising ID and App events (such as install, tutorial completed, free trial started, and subscription with currency and value) to measure and optimize our ad campaigns. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase Analytics receives usage events and App identifiers for statistics. Firebase privacy.
  • Google Ads receives conversion events from Firebase (such as first conversation and subscription) to measure and optimize our ad campaigns. Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information for money. However, sharing your advertising ID and App events with Meta, Google AdMob, and Google Ads for advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under US state privacy laws. You can opt out at any time (see Section 9). We do not sell or share the personal information of users we know are under 18.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or users' safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the App.

5. Where your data is processed

Our server is located in the United States (Google Cloud). We are based in Brazil, and our partners may process data in other countries. We use providers that offer appropriate data protection safeguards.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data, preferences, and custom characters: while your account exists.
  • Voice recordings: not kept; discarded after transcription.
  • Purchase records: for the period required by tax and consumer law (up to 5 years).
  • Usage and advertising data held by partners: according to each partner's retention periods.

When you delete your account, we delete the data linked to it within 30 days, except data we must keep by law. We may keep statistics that are aggregated or anonymized and can no longer identify you.

7. Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the App under Config > Account > Delete account, or on our account deletion page.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Microphone and notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Do Not Sell or Share: see Section 9.

8. Children and teens

The App is for people aged 16 and older and is not directed to children. On first launch we ask for your birth year, and where Google Play provides an age signal we use it as well. Users under 16 cannot use the App. For users aged 16 and 17, we do not send App events or the advertising ID to Meta, we tell Google AdMob to treat them as under the age of consent, and we deny advertising signals in Firebase. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email elderpv18@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Your US state privacy rights

If you live in California or in another US state with a consumer privacy law (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, and others), you may have the right to:

  • Know and access the personal information we collected about you, the categories of sources, the purposes, and the categories of third parties we share it with;
  • Delete your personal information;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Get a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising;
  • Not be discriminated against for using these rights. We will not deny you the App, charge a different price, or give a lower quality of service because you used them.

We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and we do not use personal information for decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

To opt out, open the App and tap Config > Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information (shown to users in the United States), or email elderpv18@gmail.com with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share" and the email of your account. When you opt out, the App turns on Meta's Limited Data Use mode, stops sending your advertising ID and App events to Meta, denies advertising signals in Firebase and Google Ads, and lets you update your Google AdMob privacy choices. Your choice is saved on your device; if you reinstall the App or use another device, set it again.

How to make a request

Email elderpv18@gmail.com from the email of your Google account, or tell us which account it is. We will verify your request by confirming you control that account, and we will respond within 45 days (we may extend this once by 45 more days if needed, and we will tell you). You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of the agent's authorization and verify your identity directly.

Appeals: if we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with the subject "Privacy Appeal". We will answer within the time required by your state's law. If you are not satisfied, you can contact your state Attorney General.
